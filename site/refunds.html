Refund policy

Effective September 3, 2026

A founding pre-order is refundable any time before the first beta is delivered, and for 30 days after delivery.

How to request a refund

Reply to your Stripe payment receipt or use the support contact shown on it. Include the purchase email and payment date. Do not post payment details in a public GitHub issue.

Timing

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method. Banks and card networks control how long the credit takes to appear after Stripe processes it.

Project cancellation

If development of the paid Mac app is cancelled before a first beta is delivered, every remaining unrefunded Founding Edition pre-order will be refunded automatically.

Free CLI

The free open-source CLI is not affected by a refund and remains available under the MIT License.
