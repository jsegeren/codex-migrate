What we are building

  • Scheduled, client-encrypted backup of supported local Codex history to off-device storage.
  • A last-verified-backup receipt and visible failures, so a silent missed run is not called protection.
  • A clean-Mac recovery drill: find a version, read or export it, and recover what the format safely permits.
  • Organization-controlled recovery and device-health visibility, with governed access to employee content.

The exact gap matters
