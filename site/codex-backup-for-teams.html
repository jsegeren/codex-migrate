What we are building

The exact gap matters

OpenAI’s Codex guidance for administrators says users’ workstations keep Codex inputs and outputs, while ChatGPT Enterprise stores its inputs and outputs in OpenAI’s cloud. It also describes Codex audit records available for up to 30 days—not a demonstrated restore of complete local work. Remote handoff can move a chat between connected hosts, but it is not an independent backup. Account-saved ChatGPT-app chats are another case.
