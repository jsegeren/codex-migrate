Codex Vault · team pilot inquiry

Same account. New Mac. Missing Codex chats.

That happened to Joshua. He signed in with the same OpenAI account on a new Mac, but his local Codex conversations did not appear. ChatGPT makes account-wide chat history feel normal; local Codex work can break that expectation. Those chats and discussions are not part of the Git repo either.

OpenAI’s local Codex guidance says workstations keep Codex inputs and outputs and organizations are responsible for local retention. If a developer’s Mac disappeared tonight, could your company recover the conversations, decisions, and unfinished work on it? We are building private, off-device business backup and recovery for that situation.

What exists today

The signed $49 Mac beta searches supported local Codex history and creates encrypted, versioned backups in a folder you choose. It does not send your conversations to Segeren Studio. It does not yet capture content stored only in Codex’s separate paginated-history database. It is not an organization-wide hosted backup or a guaranteed disaster-recovery service.

What we want to prove with teams

Private off-device backups, visible backup health across enrolled Macs, and a tested way for an authorized company representative to recover work after device loss or offboarding. Content should be encrypted before upload; backup-health access must not silently grant access to employee conversations. Search and aggregate reporting are possibilities, not promises.

Hosted team backup is in development and is not for sale yet. We will not call it protected until an off-device backup and independent clean-Mac restore both pass. No compliance certification or response-time guarantee is being offered.

Tell us what your team needs

If your team uses Codex on Macs, email Joshua with your approximate seat count, how you protect local history now, and the recovery scenario that worries you most. Do not send credentials, transcripts, or repository contents. We are speaking with prospective paid pilot teams; there is no commitment or charge for this conversation.
