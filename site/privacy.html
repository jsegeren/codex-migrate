Privacy policy

Effective September 4, 2026

The migration software does not upload your Codex data to us. Transfers run directly between Macs you control.

What the software collects

The open-source CLI and local dashboard include no analytics or telemetry. Segeren Studio does not receive your conversations, repositories, credentials, migration inventory, or verification report through the software.

Website analytics

Google Analytics is optional on this public website. The Google tag is not loaded until you select “Accept analytics.” If accepted, it helps us understand page views, traffic sources and campaigns, approximate city or region, device and browser type, returning visits, clicks to the free CLI and You.one, and successful launch-email requests. It does not receive your name, email address, Codex conversations, repositories, migration inventory, credentials, or local dashboard activity. We do not enable ads personalization or use this choice to enroll you in marketing.

For visitors who consent here and have separately consented to this association through a signed-in Google account, Google Signals may add aggregate, thresholded demographic, interest, and cross-device insights. This does not identify an individual visitor to us, and these reports may not be available when traffic is too low.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser’s local storage. After acceptance, Google Analytics may set Codex Migrate-specific first-party measurement cookies for up to 14 months and refresh their expiry when you return. Select change analytics preferences to review the choice. Choosing “No thanks” prevents the Google tag from loading and removes those product-specific cookies from this site. Google may process consented website-usage data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Vercel hosts the site and its signup endpoint and may process standard request information such as IP address, device information, requested URL, and timestamps to deliver and protect it. The signup endpoint is rate limited to reduce abuse.

Purchase data

Checkout is not open yet. When sales open, Stripe will process checkout and payment information on our behalf. We will receive transaction records and contact details needed to confirm your purchase, deliver access, provide support, prevent fraud, and process refunds. We will not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Launch emails and early-build requests

When you submit the launch form and give consent, Vercel processes your email address and sends the request through Twilio SendGrid to Joshua Segeren’s inbox. The message includes your email, consent, submission time, and the website origin. Josh manages these requests personally; there is no separate subscriber database or automated newsletter. We use the address for the requested launch notice and any necessary confirmation, not unrelated marketing. Signup success means SendGrid accepted the message, not a guarantee of inbox delivery.

Early-build and support links open your email app. Those messages are processed by your email provider and ours and handled individually. No payment is required. Email Josh to opt out or request removal. Launch requests are kept until the notice has been sent or you ask for removal, except records needed for abuse prevention or legal obligations. Do not send credentials, conversations, or repository contents. Sending an early-build request does not guarantee access.

How data is shared

We share data only with service providers needed to operate the site, consented website analytics, email, and future checkout, including Vercel, Google Analytics, Twilio SendGrid, our mailbox provider, and Stripe, or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and security

Purchase records are retained as needed for delivery, support, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. We use the access controls and security features provided by our hosting and payment providers. No internet system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can use the free CLI without buying anything or creating an account with us. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your contact or purchase-related information where legally available, email Joshua Segeren privately. Do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
