Privacy policy

Effective September 3, 2026

The migration software does not upload your Codex data to us. Transfers run directly between Macs you control.

What the software collects

The open-source CLI and local dashboard include no analytics or telemetry. Segeren Studio does not receive your conversations, repositories, credentials, migration inventory, or verification report through the software.

Website data

This static website does not set our own analytics or advertising cookies. Vercel hosts the site and may process standard request information such as IP address, device information, requested URL, and timestamps to deliver and protect it.

Purchase data

Checkout is not open yet. When sales open, Stripe will process checkout and payment information on our behalf. We will receive transaction records and contact details needed to confirm your purchase, deliver access, provide support, prevent fraud, and process refunds. We will not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Launch emails and early-build requests

The contact links open your email app; clicking them does not subscribe you or send a message automatically. If you send a launch-notification request, Joshua Segeren receives your sender address and message in his email inbox and uses them to send the requested launch notice. Early-build and support requests are handled individually. Email is processed by your email provider and ours; there is no separate signup-form service on this page.

No payment is required. Reply to opt out of launch notifications or request removal from the launch list. Do not send credentials, conversations, or repository contents. Sending an early-build request does not guarantee access.

How data is shared

We share purchase and website data only with service providers needed to operate the site and checkout, including Vercel and Stripe, or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and security

Purchase records are retained as needed for delivery, support, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. We use the access controls and security features provided by our hosting and payment providers. No internet system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can use the free CLI without buying anything or creating an account with us. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your contact or purchase-related information where legally available, email Joshua Segeren privately. Do not post sensitive information in a public issue.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
