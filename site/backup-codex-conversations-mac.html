Daily protection by default

During the first backup, choose daily automatic backup or manual-only backup. Daily is the recommended default. After the first snapshot verifies, Vault installs a private macOS schedule that creates another verified snapshot every 24 hours, even when the app is closed.

Your storage, not ours

Pick an empty local folder or a folder synced by OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or another provider. Segeren Studio does not host or receive your conversations. Vault encrypts transcript content on your Mac before writing it to the destination.

Vault identifies common cloud-sync locations and explains whether a selected folder appears local-only, cloud-synced, or uncertain. That is guidance—not a guarantee that the provider has finished uploading every file—so keep the sync client healthy and retain the recovery key.

Versioned and verified

Each snapshot is published only after every encrypted object verifies. New versions do not silently replace the last good copy. The encryption key stays in macOS Keychain, and the first backup displays a recovery key once for you to save in a password manager.

What gets backed up

Vault protects supported active and archived local Codex conversation transcript trees. It deliberately excludes authentication, installation identity, SSH keys, repositories, settings, logs, caches, and runtime locks. Use the migration workflow when you need the broader working environment.

Important: Vault can protect only content Codex actually wrote to disk. It cannot reconstruct turns that were never persisted. Keep an independent backup until you have verified your setup.
