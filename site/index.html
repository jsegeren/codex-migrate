Straight answers

Before you buy.

Is the backup really automatic?

Yes. During your first backup, daily automatic backup is selected by default; you can choose manual-only instead. After that first snapshot verifies, a private macOS LaunchAgent adds one encrypted snapshot every 24 hours—even when the app is closed—while the Mac and chosen Vault folder are available. Existing snapshots are kept. This is scheduled backup, not continuous sync.

Where does my data go?

Vault writes encrypted snapshots to the local, OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or other cloud-sync folder you choose. Segeren Studio does not host or receive them. Website analytics never receives your Codex conversations. The app itself has no telemetry.

What is backed up versus migrated?

Vault protects active and archived conversation transcripts. Full Mac migration also moves selected configuration, skills, automations, repositories, branches, worktrees, and unfinished files. Authentication and installation identity are deliberately excluded.

Can I try the Mac beta?

Yes. The signed, notarized beta is $49 one time for Apple silicon Macs, with best-effort support and a 30-day refund policy. The complete CLI and source remain free. Windows and cross-platform transfers are not supported today.
